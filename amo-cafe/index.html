Our Story

Located in the heart of Manhattan, AMO Café is more than just a place to grab a coffee. We are a destination. We believe that a café should be an experience—a blend of beautiful aesthetics, carefully crafted products, and a vibrant community.

We saw a problem: cafés offer great drinks but lack interactivity and community engagement. Our solution? Creating a space where you don't just consume—you create. That's why we introduced our signature Baking Competition.

The AMO Baking Competition
